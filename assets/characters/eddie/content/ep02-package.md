What if you woke up tomorrow and could hear everyone's thoughts? Every secret, every opinion… every random little idea.

Eddie gets the power and we follow it day by day: knowing what everyone wants, then a crowded café where 20 minds talk at once, a best friend's honest opinion, and the one quiet person in a noisy world. By Day 30, Eddie learns what's REALLY going on in people's heads (and in his own).

💬 Would you want this power? And should Eddie find out what happens if you never feel pain next? Tell us below.

🔔 Subscribe to Stick With Eddie for a new "What If" every week.

📚 SOURCES & NOTES
• Mind-wandering about 47% of waking hours: Killingsworth & Gilbert (2010), "A Wandering Mind Is an Unhappy Mind," Science.
• 6,000+ distinct thoughts a day: Tseng & Poppenk (2020), Nature Communications.
• The spotlight effect / embarrassing T-shirt study: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Intrusive thoughts in ~94% of people across 13 countries: Radomsky et al. (2014), Journal of Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders.
• People with little or no inner voice: Nedergaard & Lupyan (2024), Psychological Science.
• The cocktail party effect: Cherry (1953), Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
• Hearing thoughts is fiction, a thought experiment built on real research.
