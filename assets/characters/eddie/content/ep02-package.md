📚 SOURCES & NOTES
• Mind-wandering about 47% of waking hours: Killingsworth & Gilbert (2010), "A Wandering Mind Is an Unhappy Mind," Science.
• 6,000+ distinct thoughts a day: Tseng & Poppenk (2020), Nature Communications.
• The spotlight effect / embarrassing T-shirt study: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Intrusive thoughts in ~94% of people across 13 countries: Radomsky et al. (2014), Journal of Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders.
• People with little or no inner voice: Nedergaard & Lupyan (2024), Psychological Science.
• The cocktail party effect: Cherry (1953), Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
• Hearing thoughts is fiction: a story built on real research.
