## 1. Channel identity

### Name: **Stick With Eddie** (recommended)
- It's a pun on *stick*man and an invitation to "stick with" him through every scenario.
- The name is about **Eddie, not one topic**, so it still fits if you expand beyond "What If" later (Day 1 to Day 30, history, survival...).
- It's short, easy to say and easy to spell.
- **Handle:** `@stickwitheddie`. A vidIQ lookup found no existing channel with this handle, but only YouTube can confirm it's free when you claim it.

| Alternative | Why it might suit | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Eddie's What If | Says exactly what the channel is | Ties the channel to one format |
| Eddie Finds Out | Makes curiosity the promise | Less punny, less memorable |
| What If Eddie | Keyword-first | Reads awkwardly |

### Tagline
**What if? Eddie finds out.**

### Profile picture
`profile-800.png`: Eddie's happy face on yellow. It still reads as a face at tiny sizes, and the yellow stands out in a sidebar.

### Banner
`banner-2560x1440.png`. The name and tagline sit inside YouTube's 1546×423 safe area, so they show on phone, desktop and TV.

## 3. Channel setup checklist

- [ ] **Audience: "No, it's not made for kids."** Cartoon style doesn't make a channel "for kids". Marking it for kids turns off comments, notifications and personalized ads.
- [ ] **Category:** Education. The videos explain real science through a story.
- [ ] **Upload defaults:** paste the description template from section 5, language English, category Education.
- [ ] **Altered/synthetic content disclosure:** not needed. The animation is clearly unrealistic, and the narrator doesn't imitate a real person. Revisit this if you ever use a realistic AI voice clone of a real person.
- [ ] **Watermark:** the profile picture, shown from the end of the video.
- [ ] **Channel trailer:** use the vertical Short (or the first 60s of episode 1) for non-subscribers.
- [ ] **Playlists**, one per content bucket: *What If Your Body…* · *What If the World…* · *Day 1 to Day 30*.
- [ ] **Links:** add your socials once they exist (the same name everywhere, e.g. TikTok/Instagram `@stickwitheddie`).

### Content buckets (formats that repeat without being templates)
| Bucket | Example next videos |
|---|---|
| **What If Your Body…** | What If You Stopped Feeling Pain? · What If You Aged Backwards? · What If Your Body Ran on Sunlight? |
| **What If Your Mind…** | What If You Could Hear Everyone's Thoughts? (teased in ep 1) · What If You Could Only Tell the Truth? |
| **What If the World…** | What If the Internet Disappeared for 30 Days? · What If Money Stopped Existing? |
| **Day 1 to Day 30** | Day 1 to Day 30: Living Without Your Phone · Day 1 to Day 30: Only Water |

Keep each episode's arc, twist and structure different from the others. YouTube's July 2026 "inauthentic content" policy targets channels whose uploads look like one template with the nouns swapped.

## 6. Accuracy flag before publishing ⚠️

The script says scientists *discovered* the brain's "night-shift cleaning crew". That's based on the 2013 *Science* study above. A **2024 study (Miao et al., *Nature Neuroscience*)** reported that brain clearance was **not** higher during sleep in mice, so this is an active scientific debate. To stay accurate, consider changing the line to:
> "Some scientists think that during deep sleep, your brain runs a kind of night-shift cleaning crew…"

Changing it is one line in `ep01-never-sleep.beats.mjs` plus a re-render.
