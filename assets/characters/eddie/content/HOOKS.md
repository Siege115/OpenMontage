# Hook + story playbook (Stick With Eddie)

Patterns the channel uses for the first line of a Short / the first 5 s of an episode.

| # | Pattern | Eddie version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is it possible to [goal] in [tight time]? | "Is it possible to survive 30 days hearing everyone's thoughts?" |
| 2 | Say the exact time out loud: "It's currently [time], and I just promised myself I'd [stake] by [deadline]" | "It's 3:07 a.m. on day 12 without sleep, and you just promised yourself you'd stay awake until sunrise." |
| 3 | I don't know who needs to hear this, but… | "I don't know who needs to hear this, but nobody at that party was looking at you." |
| 4 | [x] days ago I… (beats "today is day one of…") | "Twenty-one days ago, you started hearing everyone's thoughts." |
| 6 | If you've been [painful moment]… | "If you've been lying awake replaying something dumb you said… give me 30 seconds." |
| R | Concrete action in the first 4 s, then "You see…" (Ricks Clips) | "He heard his best friend think one word… and never played poker again. You see…" |

Rules
- Open in the past or mid-action, never at "day one". Specific numbers and times beat vague ones.
- Modern story arc: start high, then alternate dip -> spike every 8-15 s (a new question, twist or quiz), each spike a little
  lower than the first, and end on the payoff. No flat stretch longer than ~12 s; re-hook before every scene change.
- Shorts: 13-35 s, payoff in the last line, last frame points to the full video.
